STUDENT PREDICTOR SYSTEM: KEY QUESTIONS TO ASK

Essential Data Points & Questions to Predict Academic Performance, Dropout Risk, and Placement Success

📝🎓

1. Academic & Past Performance

  • 📄 What is your current/past CGPA or percentage?
  • 🗓️ What is your overall attendance percentage?
  • 📋 What are your recent quiz & assignment scores?
  • 📑 Do you have any active or previous backlogs?
  • ✏️ What was your entrance or prior standardized test score?
📖💡

2. Study Habits & Engagement

  • ⏰ How many hours do you spend on self-study daily?
  • 💻 How often do you use the LMS & study portals?
  • 🙋 How actively do you ask questions & participate in class?
  • 🛠️ How many hours per week on extracurricular activities?
  • 👥 Do you study individually or in peer groups?
🛌⏳

3. Behavioral & Lifestyle Factors

  • 💤 How many hours of sleep do you get on average?
  • 📱 How much daily screen time is non-academic?
  • ⚡ How would you rate your stress & motivation levels?
  • ⏱️ Do you struggle with procrastination or missing deadlines?
  • 💜 How is your physical health and overall well-being?
